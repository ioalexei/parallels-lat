Irwin Loy, writing for The New Humanitarian, highlights the trend of humanitarian organisations removing references to climate change 
from their websites: 

[Aid groups are erasing climate change from their websites](https://www.thenewhumanitarian.org/news/2025/04/24/aid-groups-are-erasing-climate-change-their-websites)
>  Feeling Trump pressure, some agencies are editing, downplaying, or deleting climate language. 

He also notes organisations removing reference to 'gender equality'.
