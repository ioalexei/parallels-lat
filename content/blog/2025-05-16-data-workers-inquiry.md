[How we made Data Workers' Inquiry](https://data-workers.org/about/how-to-dwi/)

A nice write-up of a workers' inquiry project with data workers, researching the work that goes into training AI models (such as labelling datasets). 
The inquiries are [collected on the site](https://data-workers.org/#Inquiries) and include workers from Kenya, Syria, Latin America 
and Germany speaking about their working conditions.  

Among various outputs on the site, the article mentions that the project led to a [Data Labelers Association](https://datalabelers.org/about/) in Kenya.
