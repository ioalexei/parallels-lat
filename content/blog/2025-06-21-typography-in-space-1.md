I've been going down the rabbit hole of the NASA photo archives on 
archive.org and enjoyed these two photos showcasing the use of Helvetica 
on the Atlantis space shuttle. 

![Typography on Atlantis shuttle in orbit, 2002]({attach}/images/iss005e17705-atlantis-typography-1.jpg)
_Typography on Atlantis shuttle in orbit, 2002. Source: [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/iss005e17705)_

![Typography on Atlantis shuttle wing, 2002]({attach}/images/iss005e17718-atlantis-typography-2.jpg)
_Typography on Atlantis shuttle wing, 2002. Source: [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/iss005e17718)_

There's more detail on NASA's design and typography guidance in [this 1976 design manual](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/nasa_graphics_manual_nhb_1430-2_jan_1976.pdf) 
which includes 	examples for spacecraft markings (p.56).

![Space shuttle markings page, NASA design guide 1976]({attach}/images/nasa_design-guide-spacecraft-markings.png)
_Space shuttle markings page, NASA design guide 1976. Source: [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/nasa_graphics_manual_nhb_1430-2_jan_1976.pdf)_
